# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Ethiopian Telegram users playing for real money on mobile, looking for quick casual games. They access the game through Telegram's Mini App browser on their phones, often on variable network quality. Trust is a primary concern — they need to feel the platform is legitimate and their money is safe.

## Product Purpose

Melbete Bingo is a real-time multiplayer Bingo platform for Telegram. It delivers 75-ball and 90-ball variants with server-verified claims, an auditable random number generator, and real-money deposit/withdrawal flows. Success means players can join a game, play a round, and cash out in under 2 minutes — all without leaving Telegram.

## Brand Personality

trustworthy · playful · vibrant

The brand should feel like a legitimate, modern game — not a sketchy betting site. Colorful and energetic enough for a game, but restrained and professional enough to inspire financial trust.

## Anti-references

- Fake gambling / scammer sites — no flashing "win big now!" tropes, no shiny badges, no countdown pressure tricks
- Should NOT look like a shady betting platform; must feel transparent and auditable

## Design Principles

- **Trust through transparency** — Show the draw history, RNG state, and transaction trail. Nothing hidden.
- **Mobile-first, thumb-friendly** — Every tap target within reach of a thumb on a phone screen. No hover-dependent interactions.
- **Playful confidence** — Vibrant accent colors and celebratory moments, but never garish. A game that takes itself seriously enough to handle money.
- **Clarity over cleverness** — Game state, balances, and available actions must be immediately readable at a glance.
- **Fast & lightweight** — Quick-loading even on spotty connections. Every page view actionable within 2 seconds.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum with color-blind safe palette considerations. Essential for a money-handling app where misreading a number or button has real cost.
